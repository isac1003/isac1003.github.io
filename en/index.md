# Max Consulting Co., Ltd.

Max Consulting Co., Ltd. (맥스컨설팅) is a Korean management consulting firm founded in **1993**,
based in Anyang, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea.

The firm specializes in three areas: **management and strategy consulting** for small and
medium-sized enterprises, **research and survey services** commissioned by government agencies
and corporations, and **operation of government-funded startup support programs**.

This website provides daily Korean-language briefings on Korean government startup and SME
support programs, and insights on management trends for Korean SMEs.

Contact: yj@maxconsulting.or.kr
