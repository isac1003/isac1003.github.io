# Max Consulting Co., Ltd.

Max Consulting Co., Ltd. (맥스컨설팅) is a Korean management consulting firm founded in **April 1982**,
based in Anyang, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea. Since 2010 alone, the firm has completed
469 consulting and research projects, mostly for Korean government agencies and public institutions.

The firm specializes in four areas: **strategy and marketing consulting** for small and
medium-sized enterprises, **R&D planning and technology commercialization**, **startup
acceleration and investment attraction support** (registered accelerator since 2020), and
**public program evaluation and policy research** — with particular depth in the
agri-food sector.

This website provides daily Korean-language briefings on Korean government startup and SME
support programs, and insights on management trends for Korean SMEs.
